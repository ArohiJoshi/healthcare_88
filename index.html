Common Laboratory Tests

CBC
Complete Blood Count used to check different blood cell measurements.
HbA1c
A test used to understand average blood glucose levels.
LFT
Liver Function Test used to assess liver-related health indicators.
KFT
Kidney Function Test used to assess kidney-related health indicators.
ECG
A test that records the electrical activity of the heart.

Laboratory Test Details

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Purpose: CBC provides information about different blood cell measurements.

Sample Required: Blood sample.

Preparation Instructions: Follow the preparation instructions provided by the laboratory.

Normal Reference Range: Reference ranges depend on the individual parameter, age and laboratory.

Report Delivery Time: Usually within 24 hours.

Important Instructions

Fasting Requirements: Fasting requirements depend on the test ordered. Confirm with the laboratory before testing.

Please bring your prescription or previous reports if advised by your doctor.
